GharChalo is deployed (all data is simulated).

Open the app:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "gharchalo.fullname" . }}-web 8080:{{ .Values.web.service.port }}
  then visit http://localhost:8080

Check the pods:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
